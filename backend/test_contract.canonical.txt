MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
This Master Services Agreement is entered into by and between Service Provider and Client.
1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 Definitions.
In this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings set forth below:
"Client" means the entity entering into this Agreement.
"Service Provider" means the service provider entity entering into this Agreement.
"Losses" means any claims, damages, liabilities, losses, costs, and expenses including reasonable legal fees.
2. TERM AND TERMINATION
2.1 Term.
This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for one year.
2.2 Termination.
Either party may terminate this Agreement on ninety days written notice to the other party.
3. SERVICES
3.1 Provision of Services.
Service Provider will provide the Services described in applicable Statements of Work.
3.2 Standards.
Service Provider shall perform Services in a professional and workmanlike manner.
4. FEES AND PAYMENT
4.1 Invoicing.
Client shall pay all undisputed invoices within thirty days of receipt.
4.2 Taxes.
Fees are exclusive of applicable taxes.


5. CONFIDENTIALITY
5.1 Duty.
Receiving Party shall protect Confidential Information with the same degree of care as its own.
6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
6.1 Ownership.
Client owns all Client Data and Deliverables.
7. WARRANTIES
7.1 Mutual Warranties.
Each party warrants it has full power and authority to enter into this Agreement.
8. COMPLIANCE
8.1 Applicable Law.
Both parties shall comply with all applicable laws and regulations.
9. INDEMNIFICATION
9.1 Client Indemnity.
Client shall indemnify Service Provider against claims arising from Client Data.
9.2 Provider Indemnity.
The Service Provider shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Client and its officers, directors, and employees against any third-party claims, Losses, damages, or costs alleging that the Services infringe any intellectual property right. The Service Provider gives an uncapped IP indemnity, subject to the carve-outs in clause 9.3.
9.3 Exceptions.
Service Provider shall have no obligation under clause 9.2 for claims arising from modifications made by Client.
10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
10.1 Consequential Damages.
Neither party will be liable for indirect or consequential damages.
10.2 Aggregate Cap.
Liability of the Client and Service Provider is capped at the fees paid in the preceding twelve months.
